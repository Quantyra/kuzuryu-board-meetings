# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful,
professional, and free from harassment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code.
- Assume good faith while still asking for clear evidence.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sharing credentials, private meeting records, or sensitive operational data.
- Disruptive behavior that prevents productive collaboration.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, decline contributions, or restrict participation
when behavior conflicts with this code of conduct.
